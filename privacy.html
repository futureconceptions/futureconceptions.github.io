Privacy Policy

ClipFarm Studio · Effective 22 September 2026

1. Controller

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Future Conceptions, Germany, e-mail futureconceptions.clips@gmail.com (full legal notice in the Terms of Service).

2. What the Tool processes

ClipFarm Studio connects to social media accounts that we operate ourselves. When an account is connected through the platform's login, the platform sends us:

  • Basic account information (TikTok: open ID, display name, avatar via the user.info.basic scope; YouTube: channel ID) – used only to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token – used to upload and publish videos on that account (video.upload, video.publish / youtube.upload).

The Tool does not access messages, followers, contacts, viewing history, or any data about other users. It does not track visitors of this website and sets no cookies.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Processing serves one purpose: publishing videos we produced to accounts we operate, and keeping a record of what was published (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in running our own content operation; for the operator's own accounts also Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Storage and location

Tokens and publishing records are stored locally on the operator's own computer in Germany. They are not transferred to third parties and not sold. Videos are transmitted directly to the platform (TikTok Pte. Ltd. / TikTok Technology Limited, Google Ireland Limited) whose own privacy policies then apply: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy, policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Retention

Access tokens are kept until the connection is revoked or the account is retired, and are deleted then. Publishing records are kept for as long as the related campaign requires reporting, at most 3 years, unless statutory retention obligations require longer.

6. Revoking access

You can withdraw the Tool's access at any time in the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Withdrawal takes effect immediately.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – for us the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen. To exercise your rights, e-mail futureconceptions.clips@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when the Tool's data processing changes; the effective date above reflects the current version.
