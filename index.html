ClipFarm Studio

A private publishing tool for short-form video

ClipFarm Studio is an internal tool used by a small content team to prepare and publish short-form videos to the social media accounts the team operates. Clips are edited and quality-checked locally; the tool then delivers each approved video, with its caption, to the connected account through the platform's official API.

What it does

  • Cuts and captions short clips from licensed source footage on the team's own computer.
  • Holds each clip for a human quality review before anything is published.
  • Uploads approved clips to accounts that have explicitly authorized the tool (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Keeps a local record of what was posted where, for campaign reporting.

What it does not do

  • It never reads messages, followers, or any other user's data.
  • It never likes, comments, follows, or interacts on anyone's behalf.
  • It never creates accounts or stores passwords; access is granted through the platform's own login and can be revoked there at any time.
